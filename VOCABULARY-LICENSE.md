# Vocabulary data: attribution and license

The word and Korean-meaning data in `vocabulary-data.json` is an adapted selection released under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Attributions:

- [Open Language Profiles / CEFR-J](https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj), CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5 compiled by Yukio Tono, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies, and Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 Version 1.0. Used for headword/part-of-speech/level cross-checking and the revised general-vocabulary selection. CEFR-J permits research and commercial use with attribution; Octanove C1/C2 is CC BY-SA 4.0. These are reference profiles, not a certification of learner ability or a school-grade equivalence.
- 2026-11-02 revision: all 4,911 deployed Korean meanings were reviewed entry by entry by the coding assistant, with 2,368 corrections relative to the source dictionary. This is an AI-assisted editorial review, not certification by a human lexicographer. Specialist terms, proper-name risks, unsuitable usages, and selected redundant variants were excluded. The former bank is not shipped as vocabulary. Review decisions and reference parts of speech are recorded in `data/vocabulary-review.json`. Corrections/adaptations remain CC BY-SA 4.0.

- [koreader-dicts English–Korean dictionary](https://github.com/DanielGregorini/koreader-dicts), `en-ko` release built on 2026-09-11. Its source materials include Princeton WordNet 3.0 and English Wiktionary data extracted through [kaikki.org / wiktextract](https://kaikki.org/).
- [Open English–Korean Dictionary](https://github.com/jhseo1211/open-english-korean-dict), used for Korean meanings and CEFR/frequency-based selection of the added 입문 and 최상급 word banks. Its listed source materials include the CEFR-J Wordlist, NGSL, and NAWL.
- [open-dict-data/ipa-dict](https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict), `data/en_US.txt`, used for American English IPA pronunciations. The project releases this material under MIT and credits a modified CMUdict-IPA and syllabify for the English (US) data.
- [Open English Pronouncing Dictionary](https://github.com/JackDanger/open-english-pronouncing-dictionary), used for 107 fallback pronunciations. This adapted subset is from a corpus released under CC BY-SA 4.0; its README lists source corpora and their licenses.
- [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/pronunciation/english/screensaver) (screensaver), [Collins English Dictionary](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/dictionary/english/enneahedron) (enneahedron), and [English Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/enneagon) (enneagon), used to check three pronunciations absent from the datasets above.
