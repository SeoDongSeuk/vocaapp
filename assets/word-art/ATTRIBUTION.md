# Word illustration artwork

Twemoji graphics by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, maintained by jdecked and contributors.

- Source: https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji
- Graphics license: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The original vector graphics are bundled locally without modification. Word-to-image selections, multi-icon scenes, number labels, and the decorative card background were composed for Word Challenge. These picture clues use familiar symbols; they are not literal illustrations of every possible sense of a word.

The same source SVG content is embedded in `data/starter-artwork.json` for use with react-native-svg in Expo Go. No image service or external request is used while studying.
